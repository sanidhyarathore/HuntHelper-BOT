PATCH 2 — cost reduction. Copy all .py files into your tgjobs folder.

Your .env, profile.yaml and data\jobs.db are NOT in this zip.
The database upgrades itself automatically on next run.

FIRST, run this (free, instant):
  .venv\Scripts\python insights.py cache

It tells you whether prompt caching will actually engage. If it says
TOO SMALL, caching is silently ignored and you pay full price — add
more detail to profile.yaml until it clears.

  Repost skip     Identical postings across channels are now detected
                  by content fingerprint BEFORE any API call.
  Negative filter Obvious engineering titles skipped without paying to
                  read them. Multi-role digests are never dropped.
  Prompt caching  Your profile moved into a cached system block. After
                  the first call of a run it bills at roughly 10%.
